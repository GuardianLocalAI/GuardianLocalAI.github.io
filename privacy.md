# Guardian — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 19 October 2026**

## Overview

Guardian is a privately operated, privacy-first AI assistant designed to run primarily on local hardware.

This policy describes Guardian's integration with Google Calendar and Gmail.

## Information Guardian Accesses

When explicitly authorised by the user, Guardian may access:

- **Google Calendar:** Calendar events through the read-only `calendar.events.readonly` permission.
- **Gmail:** Email messages through the read-only `gmail.readonly` permission.

Guardian uses this information to respond to user-requested calendar and email queries.

## How Information Is Used

Google account information is used to provide the functionality requested by the authorised user.

Guardian does not use Google account information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Guardian does not send emails, delete messages or modify calendar events through these read-only integrations.

## Authentication and Storage

Guardian uses Google OAuth to request permission to access Google services.

OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted and associated with individual Guardian user profiles. Access tokens are used to communicate with Google's services.

Guardian is designed to operate on privately controlled infrastructure rather than a publicly hosted application server.

## Sharing and Disclosure

Guardian does not intentionally disclose Google Calendar or Gmail information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information retrieved from Google services is processed to fulfil user requests. Any additional processing or transmission depends on the Guardian features the user enables.

## User Control

Users can disconnect Google Calendar and Gmail within Guardian.

Users can also revoke access through their [Google Account security settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Data Retention and Deletion

Disconnecting a Google integration removes Guardian's stored authorisation for that integration. Google account data may also appear in locally retained conversation history or other user-controlled records.

Users should review and remove any such retained records separately when required.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer using the support email displayed on Guardian's Google OAuth consent screen.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Guardian's functionality or data-handling practices change.
